Gellphant

Challenge the claim. Check the actual work.

An anti-sycophancy review layer for claims, plans and agent output. Ask for evidence, surface uncertainty and inspect signs of apparent compliance.

What you can do

  • Challenge agreementAsk what would disprove the recommendation.
  • Inspect fake fixesReview placeholders, swallowed errors and weakened assertions.
  • Make gaps visibleSeparate evidence, assumptions and work still to verify.
Why it helps

Get a specific challenge and a verification next step before relying on a confident answer.
