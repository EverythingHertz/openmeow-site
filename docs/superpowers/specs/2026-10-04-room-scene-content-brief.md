# OpenMeow room and scene brief

Date: 2026-10-04
Status: eight-room content direction retained; visual style reset to modern offices; orange long-haired cats are the main cast
Scope: eight room establishing scenes plus arrival, each in landscape and portrait

## Purpose

Every scene should help a visitor understand an outcome they can pursue through OpenMeow. The picture must communicate a useful action using a recognizable input and result. Shared materials and characters make the world coherent; different equipment and actions make the rooms distinct.

The user approved completing the eight-room coverage and asked that every image fit its room's content and goals. This brief expands the earlier three-scene pilot. It does not authorize or claim a deployed site, operational integrations or finished video.

### Product meaning and limits

- FundMeMeow: supplied opportunity prioritization by intent, deadlines, prerequisites and blockers. Do not depict automatic award success or imply verified live discovery.
- Founder Ops: decision packets, weekly briefs, journal and follow-through. Historical OpenMeow FounderOS also spans Watch / Gate / Remember; it remains connected to observatory, review and archive rather than being reduced to a calendar.
- Subtle Lasagne and orchestration: bounded assignments, task state, returns and human checkpoints. Show pending and blocked work as well as ready work.
- Agents, skills and plugins: reusable briefs, procedures and compatible connections. Agent Mint prepares delegation contracts; it is not itself a worker runtime. The workshop assembles a kit, not an autonomous agent creature.
- /dontgaslightme: current inspected work concerns evidence-bound, authority-aware editable decks. The initial scene anchors creation in that real workflow. It does not claim one image covers every 3D, film, audio or web sub-workflow.
- Gellphant: challenge unsupported claims and apparent compliance. Discernment is the action; universal rejection, perfect truth detection and eliminated hallucinations are not claims we can make.
- Research and memory: source retrieval, linked notes and resumable handoffs. Avoid magical omniscience.
- Observatory: inspectable activity, cost awareness and receipts. Concept instruments are metaphors, not screenshots of verified live telemetry.

Ground these boundaries in the existing capability catalog, especially FOUNDER-01/02/04/07, ORCH-01/02/05/07/18, ROOT-02/03/04 and LOCAL-01/02/05. No private organization data is used as scenery.

## Visual continuity

Current direction: sleek contemporary offices, floor-to-ceiling glass, slim graphite frames, white and pale-gray surfaces, satin aluminum/chrome, light oak and restrained sage/teal accents. Neutral daylight and diffuse architectural lighting replace the previous amber glow. Optional older influences are specifically 1950s mid-century modern: simple molded-plywood chairs, tapered legs and low flat-front credenzas. Avoid brass/gold dominance, ornate joinery, library-like styling, vintage globes, busts and decorative ivy.

Most cats are orange, long-haired Norwegian Forest cats in the established OpenMeow family: ginger tabby coat, long layered cheek fur, very full cream-white chest ruff, tufted upright ears, amber eyes, pink nose and fluffy paws. Use the site's cat.webp and original tower cats as identity references. Keep adult feline proportions and a proper muzzle. Do not merely recolor a short-haired tabby. Roles are distinguished through contemporary clothing, posture, expressions and tools. Occasional other cats may provide variety; orange long-haired cats remain the default. Gellphant preserves the ivory/grey elephant, round dark glasses, small tusks, curved trunk marks and red stamp.

Distinguishing forms: minimal concierge counter; opportunity/calendar desk; task-routing board; modular capability workbench; illuminated creation table; evidence comparison table; contemporary source archive; glazed observatory workbench. Interpret each in the modern material and lighting system. Paper, tablets and screens can coexist where they make the activity clearer.

One action dominates each image. Secondary props support it. Product names, actions, readiness and links will be rendered as live web text; no essential labels belong inside generated pixels.

## Portrait contract

Each portrait is generated as a new camera composition from its selected landscape scene. Preserve the task, character identities, materials, objects needed to understand the action and their causal relationship. A scene is not accepted just because both faces fit: the input, action and result must fit too.

Use diagonals and depth to organize wide equipment into tall compositions. Do not solve the layout by clipping the key board, stamp, connector, diagram layer or source card. Keep enough quiet space for live labels without letting the action become tiny.
