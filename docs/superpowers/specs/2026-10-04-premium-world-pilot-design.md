## Intended outcome

OpenMeow is a memorable entrance to reusable tools, skills, plugins and agents. Visitors should recognize what a product helps them do and enter it directly, while the miniature world makes discovery enjoyable. Keep Gellphant and /dontgaslightme visible by name. Public destinations describe transferable capabilities; private client or company work is not a public navigation category.

The user's quality priorities are explicit: retain moving imagery on mobile and restore sharpness on both formats. A low-resolution or static-only mobile experience does not satisfy this brief.

Cross-cutting tools may appear in more than one workflow, but each has one primary destination. /dontgaslightme's current core is governed, evidence-bound editable deck creation; any wider artifact promise is a proposed extension. Gellphant reviews reasoning and overconfidence; the presentation must not promise that it eliminates hallucinations.

## Visual rules

Pale limestone, dark walnut, patinated brass, green enamel, clear and opal glass. Warm practical lamps meet slate-blue dusk. Fine fabric, felt, fur and believable contact shadows communicate a physical miniature. Preserve architectural detail; use depth of field sparingly.

A brass-and-glass central elevator connects floors. Keep five floors, dome silhouette, bay spacing and elevator location stable. Avoid repeated generic offices. Every room shows one readable task, with restrained set dressing.

Gellphant retains the established ivory/grey elephant, large round dark glasses, small tusks, curved trunk marks and red stamp. Keep the thoughtful tweed-clothed cat as a companion. Do not replace the mascot with a generic elephant.

All product names, links, status and interface labels are live HTML. Generated scenery has no essential baked-in lettering. Concept illustrations are visual references, not usable 3D geometry or proof of a common editable set.

## Pilot shot specification

### A01 — Arrival

Landscape: complete tower sits to the right, leaving calm sky for page copy. Dome and base stay inside frame. All six capability areas have distinct activities.

Portrait: separately composed camera view, complete tower much larger within the narrow frame, limited sky and foreground. Preserve the exact floor count and room assignments.

Proposed motion: a slow approach towards the tower as the elevator rises one level; practical lights are steady and a few cats make subtle movements. Do not oscillate back and forth or force users through a long introduction.

### G01 — Gellphant

Medium shot of Gellphant comparing a claim card with a source while the tabby follows the comparison. The red stamp, paper and expressions are the focal triangle. The intended action is careful review, not indiscriminate rejection.

Landscape: characters side by side with evidence visible across the table. Portrait: a closer, diagonal view down the same table, preserving both faces and the stamp.

First proposed video test: 6 seconds, 1080p, landscape, silent. Camera approaches gently; Gellphant lowers the reviewed card and sets the stamp down once, then settles. Choose a modest action to evaluate face, glasses, paws, stamp and paper stability.

### D01 — /dontgaslightme

Two cats work at an illuminated drafting table. Separate translucent chart/diagram layers, one composed presentation board and paired source cards express editable, traceable artifact creation. Source material is visible as a meaningful part of the work.

Landscape: table stretches across frame, orange tabby on the left and grey cat on the right. Portrait: a deeper angle along the same table, keeping the assembly action central. This is a new composition, not an automatic crop.

Proposed motion: the grey cat lowers one translucent diagram layer into registration while the orange cat aligns a source card. Single simple action, no morphing finished document, no invented legible UI.

## Motion and web experience

Use a shared editable 3D set for final architectural continuity where feasible. Its asset sources, character rigs, lighting and landscape/portrait cameras must be saved. AI stills and short video studies establish art direction and test feasibility first. No editable 3D master exists in this pilot yet.

Use rendered sequences in the existing web tour with lightweight navigation. Keep persistent area links and direct product entry. Scroll must remain native and touch-friendly. Reduced-motion mode provides deliberately composed stills and equivalent links; a visitor can select motion preference explicitly. Ordinary mobile browsing retains motion.

Each scene consists of an approach, one meaningful action and a stable settle. Avoid mandatory reverse travel to the exterior between every product. Navigation may jump directly to a scene without replaying the entire film.

Select landscape or portrait media using container orientation, preserving the current chapter and normalized progress on rotation. Decode the current scene and prepare the next; release distant video decoders. Do not preload the entire high-quality film suite or degrade all mobile users to the smallest rendition.

## Production quality and verification

- Produce separate 1920×1080 landscape and 1080×1920 portrait video deliverables at minimum. Catalog-supported 1080p is not proof of delivered detail: inspect actual files and motion.
- Render final 3D source masters at higher resolution where available; derive web renditions after visual comparison. Do not upscale poor source footage and call it a quality restoration.
- Initial acceptance checks: 360, 390, 430, 768 and 1440 CSS-pixel layouts; actual iOS Safari and Android Chrome playback before release; orientation change; reduced motion; keyboard; no-JS fallback; interrupted network.
- Check faces, glasses, limbs, stamp, paper edges, architecture and temporal flicker throughout the generated test. Reject structural drift rather than masking it with blur.
- Compare key frames at full display size. Measure dropped frames, seeking behavior and memory on real devices before selecting codec/rendition budgets.
- A stopped video, failed autoplay or failed request must retain a meaningful poster and usable product links.
- All product entry points are reachable without completing the cinematic tour. Touch targets are at least 44 CSS pixels and keyboard focus remains visible.
- No new production file replaces the current tour until the pilot is reviewed and its implementation is approved.
